
I've noticed that there are 3 main layers to pieces of media:

## The world layer (where)
This is the world itself.

## The action layer (what)
What happens in said world. Like the story of our characters.

## The presentation layer (how)
How the world is presented to the audience. In 3D that would be camera space.


The key with immersion is trying to place the audience in the second layer and eliminating the third one.
